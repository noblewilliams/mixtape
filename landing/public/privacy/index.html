Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

Mixtape is a personal DJ: you describe a moment, it makes you a mix. To do that it needs some of your music information and what you say to it. This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it and how to delete it.

Who we are

Mixtape is made and run by Noble Williams, an individual developer. When this page says “we”, that is who it means. For anything about your data, email hello@noblewilliams.dev.

What we collect

Your account

When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive the name, email address and account identifier that service shares with us. To keep you signed in, we also record sign-in sessions, which can include your IP address and the browser or device you used. You can also use Mixtape without signing in; we then create an anonymous account for that device.

What you tell the DJ

Your prompts, your conversations with the DJ, and the mixes and playlist drafts that come out of them. If you use voice, we receive the short audio clip so it can be turned into text.

Your music

  • Apple Music. When you sync, your library and playlists. On iPhone this can include how many times you have played each song.
  • Spotify export. If you import a Spotify data export, the archive is read on your device and is not uploaded. Only a normalised summary is sent: song identities, listening totals by day, saved music, followed artists and playlist contents, plus the country and time zone that help us match songs and dates. Private-session plays are left out unless you choose to include them.
  • Taste you share directly. Artists and songs you pick or paste, and your answers to a few taste questions.

Memories

Notes the DJ saves about your taste when you ask it to remember something. You can forget a saved note at any time.

Simple usage milestones

A few milestones, such as finishing an import or making a mix, recorded against your account with the time and whether it happened in the app or the browser. We use them to see whether Mixtape works, and report them only as counts.

How we use it

Only to make your mixes and to personalise them to your taste. We do not use your data for anything else.

No tracking, no ads, no sale

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites. Mixtape has no advertising or third-party analytics tools.

AI, and asking first

Mixtape’s DJ runs on Anthropic’s Claude. To make a mix we send it what you type or say, and the music and listening history you have added. Voice clips are transcribed by Groq. Before your first mix, Mixtape asks for your permission to do this. If we add a new AI provider or send it new kinds of data, we will ask again.

Who processes it

We use these services to run Mixtape. Each receives only what it needs for its job.

  • Cloudflare hosts the Mixtape service. Its Workers AI creates embeddings, numeric summaries of text used to match moods and songs.
  • Neon hosts our database.
  • Netlify hosts this website and the web app.
  • Anthropic runs Claude, the model behind the DJ.
  • Groq turns voice clips into text.
  • Apple, through MusicKit, provides your Apple Music library, the music catalogue and playback.
  • ReccoBeats, LRCLIB, Spotify and Deezer are looked up for public information about songs, such as audio features, lyrics and artwork. These lookups use the song’s identity, not your account or your listening.

Lyrics are processed only to understand a song’s themes. Mixtape never stores or displays lyric text.

The album artwork on Mixtape’s home page is loaded from Apple’s public catalogue.

How long we keep it

  • Your data is kept until you delete your account, or until you remove the source it came from (for example, an imported Spotify export).
  • Anonymous accounts, used without signing in, are deleted with their data after 30 days without activity.
  • Our service providers keep their own logs and backups for their standard retention periods, so a deleted item can remain in those for a limited time.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account inside Mixtape: on iPhone in Account, and on the web in Settings. Choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your mixes, library, memories and listening history from Mixtape and revokes Mixtape’s Sign in with Apple access. Usage milestones are kept only as counts with the link to you removed. Your Apple Music and Spotify libraries are not touched.

You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing hello@noblewilliams.dev from the address on your account.

Your rights

You can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law. Email hello@noblewilliams.dev and we will reply.

Children

Mixtape is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

When this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top. If a change affects what we send to an AI provider, Mixtape will ask for your permission again.
