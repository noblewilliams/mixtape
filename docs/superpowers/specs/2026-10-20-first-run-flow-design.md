## Product direction

A new listener reaches a mix without creating an account. On launch they
either ask the DJ straight away or add their music first. Sign-in is the last
page, offered after the first mix, and never required before it. Every page
asks one thing, with a headline and at most one line under it. The look is
the one-step-per-page screen the founder shared on 2026-10-20: a step
counter, a thin progress line, a rewind glyph for back, a large headline and
stacked full-width pills. Web uses the same pages in a centred column.

Anonymous listeners are real accounts. Their mixes and syncs belong to an
anonymous user tied to the device (iOS keychain) or browser (first-party
cookie). When they sign in, everything moves to the signed-in account once.

## The pages

Path A, make a mix now (three pages):

1. **Welcome.** Wordmark, cassette, "What do you want to hear?", the
   composer field with rotating example prompts and the send key. Under it,
   one pill: **Add my music first**. At the foot, a text link for returning
   listeners: **Already have an account? Sign in.**
2. **Who do you love?** Three artist fields (chips once entered), "Three
   artists is enough. The DJ borrows from the shared catalogue until your
   music is in." Continue is enabled at three. This page exists because a
   listener with nothing synced gets a corpus mix only from seeds (see "Why
   path A asks for artists").
3. **Mix.** The existing conversation and arrangement screen. The DJ's first
   reply says plainly that the picks come from the shared catalogue
   (`CORPUS_NOTICE`, unchanged). After the first mix lands, one sheet:
   **Keep this mix.** "Sign in and your mixes and music stay with you on any
   device." One pill, **Keep this mix**, which pushes the sign-in page, and a
   text link **Not now**. Provider buttons never appear inside a sheet or the
   composer panel (founder, 2026-10-20). Dismissing it once is final for the
   session (on web, the browser tab's session, so a new tab after the next mix
   shows it again); the You tab keeps a quiet **Sign in** row.

Path B, add my music first (three counted steps, then path A):

1. **Step 1 of 3, Which do you use?** One line under the headline: "Mixes
   get better when the DJ knows your music." Pills: Apple Music, Spotify.
   Text link **Skip for now** (goes to path A page 2).
2. **Step 2 of 3, How do you want to add it?**
   - Apple Music: **Sync my library** (MusicKit authorisation, the sheet is
     Apple's), **Upload an Apple export** (the existing go-deeper route).
   - Spotify: **Exportify, a few minutes**, **Spotify data export, a few
     days**. One line: "Exportify brings your playlists and likes. The data
     export adds what you actually played."
   One line on both: "You can add more later from Library."
3. **Step 3 of 3, the doing page.** Per method:
   - Apple sync: progress with counts as they arrive ("1,284 songs so far"),
     then **Done**. Failure keeps the counts and offers Retry.
   - Exportify: three instruction lines, **Open Exportify** (leaves the app)
     and **Choose files**. The existing review step (file roles, Liked Songs
     confirmation) follows unchanged.
   - Spotify data export: the existing request instructions, then
     **I’ve requested it**. The listener continues to path A page 2 and the
     Home waiting row takes over when the files arrive.
   - Apple export: the existing instructions and picker.
4. **What do you want to hear?** Path A page 1 without the add-music pill.
   One line under the field: "1,284 songs in. Ask for anything." If the sync
   produced nothing usable, path A page 2 (artists) comes first.
5. Mix, then the Keep-this-mix sheet, as in path A.

Returning anonymous listener: launch goes straight into the normal shell
(tabs on iOS, sidebar on web). The first-run pages show only when the device
has no session of any kind. The You tab for an anonymous listener shows one
row at the top, **Sign in to keep your mixes**, instead of the identity block,
and no Sign out.

Sign-in page: the existing sign-in screen with its headline changed to
"Keep your mixes." when reached from the sheet or the You row, and unchanged
when reached from the welcome foot link. Both providers, the last-used pencil
note, the reserved status line and the Apple Music foot note stay.

Step counter: only path B counts, "STEP n OF 3", because its three pages are
a bounded errand. Path A has no counter. The progress line under the counter
fills a third per step. Back is the rewind glyph (transport symbols rule,
mobile-design-preferences); on web it is the same glyph.

Cap reached: the composer stays as it is. Sending a fourth mix pushes the
sign-in page with the headline "Keep your mixes." and the line "That’s three
mixes on this device. Sign in to keep going, and to keep these." Back returns
to Home with the prompt kept in the field; the prompt is sent after sign-in.
Nothing about the cap is drawn inside the composer panel.

## Backgrounds

Every first-run page sits on a full-bleed graphic: grainy silk marble, one
prism hue per page, the same in light and dark mode (the graphic is the
theme on these pages; Mix, Home and You keep the app's gradient). Text on
them is cream, pills are cream paper with dark text, the back button is the
app's own tape shape drawn as an outline (`TapeButton` geometry: 40 pt tall,
6 pt top and 10 pt bottom radii; a 1.5 pt cream stroke, no fill, no shadow)
carrying the cream rewind glyph, and a scrim darkens the page so cream text
and the counter reach 4.5:1 everywhere. On the app's gradient screens the
same outline uses the text colour. The reference's round filled back button
is not used.

The images are generated, not photographed: a domain-warped noise field
folded into bands, lit from the band gradient, mapped to dark, mid and sheen
colours from the prism palette, then monochrome grain, rendered at 780 x 1688
(2x the 390 x 844 frame) and saved as JPEG. The generator is a short Python
script with numpy and Pillow, `generate-backgrounds.py` beside the images;
the seeds are fixed, so a regeneration reproduces the same images. Implementation ships
the JPEGs as assets (iOS asset catalogue at 2x and 3x, web `public/`) and
never draws the pattern at runtime. Reduced transparency does not affect
them; reduced motion does not either, since they are still.

## Why path A asks for artists

Today a listener with no library, no listening ledger and no seeds gets no
mix: `resolvePoolMode` returns `insufficient_seeds` and the DJ says it does
not know them yet (decision 2026-10-03, honest empty mixes). A corpus mix
needs seeds matching at least 25 enriched catalogue tracks across 3 artists
(`MIN_SEED_TRACKS`, `MIN_SEED_ARTISTS` in `server/src/dj/pool.ts`). The
artists page writes those seeds through `/me/artist-seeds` before the first
prompt is sent.

The limit this does not remove: the shared catalogue is the enriched union of
existing listeners' music, so a newcomer whose three artists are absent from
it still gets the refusal. The board shows that state honestly ("I don't
know those artists yet. Add your music, or name a few more."). The funnel
records it (`first_mix_refused`). Making a stranger's prompt work from the
Apple catalogue alone is a separate program (catalogue-seeded corpus mixes:
resolve named artists through Apple search, enrich on demand) and is not in
this spec. Decision 4 below asks whether to start it.

## Account model

Better Auth 1.7.2 ships an `anonymous` plugin, verified in
`server/node_modules/better-auth/dist/plugins/anonymous/`:

- `POST /api/auth/sign-in/anonymous` creates a `user` row with
  `is_anonymous = true`, a placeholder email and a session, and returns the
  session token the way the social sign-in does (the `bearer` plugin applies).
- `onLinkAccount({ anonymousUser, newUser, ctx })` runs after a social sign-in
  or OAuth callback that arrives with an anonymous session, before the plugin
  deletes the anonymous user. The before-hook on `/sign-in/social` carries
  the anonymous user id through the OAuth state, so the web redirect flow
  links even if the callback lands without the cookie. The after-hook
  matches `/sign-in/*` and `/callback/*`, which covers both the web redirect
  and the native id-token exchange (`client/lib/data/auth/auth_repository.dart`
  posts to `/api/auth/sign-in/social`; it must send the anonymous bearer
  token on that request).
- The anonymous user is deleted after the hook unless
  `disableDeleteAnonymousUser` is set. Deletion cascades through every
  user-keyed table, which is how rows we chose not to move disappear.

Configuration: `emailDomainName: 'anonymous.mixtape.invalid'`,
`generateName: () => 'Listener'`. Migration: `user.is_anonymous boolean not
null default false`.

When the anonymous user is created: lazily, at the first action that needs
the server (sending the first prompt, saving artist seeds, starting a sync),
not at launch. Opening the app and leaving creates no row. Gating: the first-run flow is
shown when there is no session, and stays mounted once the anonymous
session appears mid-flow until the flow hands off to the shell (the prompt
is sent). An anonymous session already present at launch goes straight to
the shell. Beyond that the client distinguishes anonymous from account only
for the You row and the cap.

### The merge

`onLinkAccount` runs one transaction. Let A be the anonymous user and N the
signed-in user.

- N was created by this sign-in (no `dj_sessions`, no `user_music_sources`,
  no `user_tracks`): repoint `user_id` from A to N on every user-keyed table.
  Nothing collides.
- N already exists with data: move `dj_sessions` (and through it messages,
  events, queue rows, seeds, versions), `dj_memories`, `funnel_events` and
  `playback_evidence`. Do not move library data (`user_tracks`,
  `user_track_library_sources`, `user_recent_track_observations`,
  `listening_days`, `user_music_profiles`, `user_artist_seeds`,
  `user_music_sources`, the three run tables and their staging rows,
  `playlist_origins`, `user_playlists`, `playlist_edit_drafts`) or settings
  (`playback_settings`, `suggestion_settings`). The existing account's
  library wins; the anonymous library goes with the cascade. The sign-in
  result tells the client `merged: 'all' | 'mixes_only'` and the client says
  "Your mixes are in. This account already had a library, so we kept that
  one." once.
- Every move skips a row whose per-user unique key N already holds
  (`dj_memories` is unique on user and note; the one-row-per-user settings
  tables, the open-run partial indexes, and the primary keys on the library
  tables). A skipped row stays on A and goes with the cascade: the account's
  own row wins. `funnel_events` move so the funnel reads as one person.
- `merged` reaches the client once on `GET /me`, not in the sign-in
  response, which the plugin owns: the hook stamps `session.merged_mode` on
  the new session inside the merge transaction, and `/me` returns and clears
  it in one statement. Values: `all` (new account took everything),
  `mixes_only` (at least one mix moved, the account's library won),
  `library_kept` (no mix moved, A's library was dropped), or null.
- The link's surface is inferred from the request: an id-token exchange is
  `ios`, the redirect callback is `web`.

The hook must be idempotent against a retried callback: when nothing is left
to move it returns `none` and writes nothing, and a deleted A makes it return
early. A failure inside the hook is logged (error name and Postgres code
only) and rethrown, so the sign-in fails and the anonymous user is not
deleted unmerged.

### Limits

- Anonymous mix cap: 3 new `dj_sessions` per anonymous user, enforced in
  `POST /sessions` (count where `user_id = A`), returning 403 with code
  `anonymous_cap`. Follow-up turns inside an existing session are not capped
  beyond today's limits.
- `POST /sign-in/anonymous`: Better Auth custom rate limit, 5 per hour per
  IP (`rateLimit.customRules`).
- Imports and syncs: the same limits as accounts. Enrichment of an anonymous
  library runs as it does for accounts, because the first mix's quality
  depends on it.
- Retention: the maintenance cron deletes anonymous users with no session
  activity for 30 days (`user.is_anonymous and max(session.updated_at) <
  now() - 30 days`). Cascade removes their rows, except `funnel_events`,
  whose `user_id` is set to null so the funnel keeps its non-converters.
  Logged in the cron line as `anonymous_expired`.
- Session life: Better Auth's default 7-day session would strand an
  anonymous listener, who has no way to sign back in. Sessions last 30 days
  and refresh daily on use, for anonymous and account sessions alike.

### What the device loses

Web: clearing site data or a private window loses the anonymous session and
with it the mixes and library. A 401 on any read signs the listener out, as
it does for accounts; for an anonymous listener that discards their only
identity, which is right once the session has expired and the retention job
will clear the rows. iOS: the keychain survives reinstall (the
stale-keychain item in the backlog stays as is). The Keep-this-mix sheet is
the mitigation; nothing else is attempted.

## Clients

iOS (`client/lib/main.dart`): the root switch becomes `unknown` (spinner),
`noSession` (first-run flow), `session` (shell). `ServiceGate` and its
once-only choice are removed; Library's **Add your music** reuses path B's
pages 1 to 3 as a pushed flow with the same counter. `authProvider` gains
`isAnonymous`. The sign-in request carries the anonymous bearer token. After
link, the client stores the new token and invalidates `authProvider` as a
sign-in does today, which resets every user-scoped provider.

Web (`web/src/RootApp.tsx`): `AuthGate` renders `FirstRun` for the no-session
and active-anonymous states, the same pages in a 420 px column over the
full-bleed image. The Better Auth React client adds `anonymousClient()`. The
`ChooseServiceDialog` modal is removed; Your music gains a quiet "Add your
music" action that opens path B's pages over the shell and returns to Your
music. Web differences, recorded 2026-10-20: Step 2 for Apple Music offers
only "Sync my library", because the web has no reader for Apple's export
file; the Apple sync page drives the same sync run the Sources panel uses,
without that panel's chrome; the Exportify and data-export instructions are
the existing full lines.

Funnel events (new, one-time per anonymous user): `first_run_opened`,
`chose_make_mix`, `chose_add_music`, `chose_service`, `chose_method`,
`sync_done`, `first_mix_made`, `first_mix_refused`, `keep_sheet_shown`,
`keep_sheet_signed_in`, `keep_sheet_dismissed`, `cap_reached`,
`account_linked_new`, `account_linked_existing`.

The interview is not in the funnel. It stays where it is, offered from Home
rows after the first mix.

## Accessibility

- Every pill and the back glyph are 44 pt targets; the step counter is
  decorative and the page title carries the step: the accessible page name is
  "Step 2 of 3, How do you want to add it?" and VoiceOver announces it on
  push. The progress line has no accessible value of its own.
- 200 % text: headlines wrap, pills grow in height, the page scrolls, the
  foot link stays after the content and never floats over it.
- Reduced motion: the cassette hubs stop, page transitions cross-fade, the
  progress line jumps instead of animating.
- Reduced transparency: pills and the sheet become opaque on the gradient.
- Sync progress is announced as a live region at most once per ten seconds.
- Nothing is colour-only: the enabled Continue pill differs by weight and
  the arrow glyph, not only by opacity.

## Tests

Server: anonymous sign-in creates a flagged user and a bearer token;
`POST /sessions` returns 403 past the cap for anonymous and never for
accounts; `onLinkAccount` moves everything into a new account; moves only
mixes, memories, funnel and evidence into an existing account with a library;
is a no-op when retried; the retention job deletes only expired anonymous
users; the rate-limit rule applies to `/sign-in/anonymous`. Client: provider
tests for the three-way root switch and `isAnonymous`; widget tests per page
(counter, enabled Continue at three artists, sheet dismiss once, cap
message); the sign-in request carries the bearer token. Web: component tests
for the same pages and for the merge notice.

## Decisions taken (founder, 2026-10-20)

1. Merge into an existing account: mixes move, the existing library wins.
2. Anonymous mix cap of 3 and 30-day retention of idle anonymous users.
3. Anonymous user created lazily at the first server action, not at launch.
4. The catalogue-seeded corpus program is not started now. The artists page
   is the floor; the refusal rate is read from `first_mix_refused`.
5. Mixes only. Album recommendations are a future feature.
6. Provider buttons live only on the sign-in page, never in a sheet or the
   composer panel.
7. First-run pages carry generated graphic backgrounds (see "Backgrounds").

Recorded in `docs/decisions.md` the same day.
