Native iOS · shape, menu and screen map · revision 1 · synthetic data

Where everything lives on the phone.

The features work. This board decides the app's shape before any screen is styled: which destinations exist, how people move between them, and which shared visual foundation every screen will be built from. Individual screens get their own boards afterwards, in the order listed at the end.

Three decisions are on the table.
  • Shape. Option A keeps Home as the only root and moves the current overflow menu into a structured You sheet. Option B introduces a three-tab bar. A is recommended.
  • Foundation. Replace the Material 3 seed-generated pink palette with the web palette as native semantic tokens, keep the system font for reading, and use Noteworthy for mix titles and the wordmark. Reuse the web cassette, stripe and icon geometry.
  • Design order. Twelve screen boards, listed at the end, each covering populated, empty, loading and failure states.

2 · Shape

The approved September 8 Home has one root and a compact overflow menu. That menu now holds seven items and the approved feature program adds at least three more, so it has to grow into something. Two ways to grow it are drawn below with the same synthetic data.

Option A · Home hub and a You sheet Recommended

Home stays the only root. The overflow button becomes an avatar that opens a bottom sheet named You, grouping Your music, the DJ's memory, listening and suggestion settings, and Account. The bottom of Home belongs to the mini-player and the composer alone.

Sign in → Choose music → (Apple: connect & sync | Spotify: import guide) → Home Home ─ composer · suggestions · Active/Archived mixes · mini-player ├─ Mix ─ Conversation ─ Arrangement ─ Now playing (local) │ └ Version history (local) · Create playlist · Send to Music └─ You (sheet) ─ Your music ─ Playlists ─ Playlist detail ─ Private edit draft │ └ Spotify import guide ─ Import sheet · Sync library ├ What the DJ knows · Listening (local) · Suggestions (local) ├ Together (planned) ─ Blends · Taste twins └ Account · Sign out
Why it fitsSessions-first is preserved: the tape closet is the app. The composer keeps its approved place and the player takes the only other pinned slot, so nothing competes for the thumb zone. Matches the web sidebar's account footer without copying desktop chrome.
What it costsYour music and the DJ's memory are one tap further away than a tab would put them. Both are setup-time or occasional, not daily, so the cost is accepted. If Together ships and turns out to be daily, reopen this decision.

Option B · Three-tab bar

Mixes, Your music and You become persistent tabs. The mini-player docks above the bar, as Apple Music does. Together would become a fourth tab.

Mixes (tab) ─ composer · suggestions · Active/Archived ─ Mix ─ Conversation ─ Arrangement … Your music (tab) ─ sources · sync · Spotify import ─ Playlists ─ Playlist detail ─ Private edit draft Together (tab, planned) ─ Blends · Taste twins You (tab) ─ What the DJ knows · Listening · Suggestions · Account · Sign out
Why it could fitFamiliar iOS music-app pattern; Your music is one tap away; a fourth tab has an obvious home when Together ships.
What it costsTwo stacked bottom surfaces take roughly 150 points from every list. It promotes setup-time screens to daily prominence and dilutes the sessions-first rule. The conversation and arrangement screens would still need to hide the bar for the keyboard, so navigation would flicker between levels.

4 · Variants that change the decision

Option A, since it is the recommendation. The same three checks apply to whichever shape is approved.

Reduced motion. Cassette hubs do not turn, the composer's rotating examples stop on the first example, the sheet and mini-player appear without slide. Every progress state is also written in text ("Replacing four songs…"), so the static reading is complete. Short phone (iPhone SE, 568 points). The suggestion card collapses to its kicker line and the list shows two rows before scrolling; nothing pinned changes height.

5 · What approval covers, and what comes next

Approving this board locks: the shape (Option A unless you choose B), the destination list and its grouping, the token set and type rules in section 3, the reuse of the web cassette, stripe and icon geometry, the pinned-surface rule (composer and mini-player only), and the Planned placeholders' positions. It covers iOS light and dark, 390-point width with the 320 and 200% checks shown, and reduced motion.

It does not approve any individual screen's final layout, the Now-playing screen, Together, or copy beyond what is shown. Nothing here is implemented; the native app still runs the Material 3 seed theme today.

Intentionally unresolved: the Android marker font, whether the You sheet opens as a half sheet or full sheet on tablet widths, and whether the suggestion card can be dismissed for a whole day from Home or only from its settings.
