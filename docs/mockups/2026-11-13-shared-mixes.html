Shared mixes

A mix gets a link. Whoever opens it sees the mix as the sender made it, with the sender’s first name, and can play it, but not prompt, edit or remix it. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-shared-mixes-design.md; plan task B0. Look and grammar come from the approved one-platform play and mix conversation boards, the profile-name page, and the web’s tape buttons. Names, songs and links are made up. Nothing here is implemented.

PlannedEvery frame is new except the system share sheet and iMessage, which are drawn approximately.
Open questions for you (my pick is drawn; say if you want the other)
  1. Glyphs. Share = next-track (pass it on), Stop sharing = stop, Remove = eject. No arrow-out-of-box share icon, per the transport rule. Alternative: the iOS share glyph for Share only, since people recognise it.
  2. Shared screen header: Back only. There is nothing to put in ⋯ (no rename, history, tape settings). Alternative: keep ⋯ with a single Remove, so it can be removed without going back to Mixes.
  3. Web page action weight. Play is the tape button, Get the app a label button, Make your own mix a quiet text link. Alternative: Make your own mix as a second label button, if growth matters more than playing here.
  4. The tape-colour preview image is the cassette alone on the tape colour, with no words or wordmark (L3). Alternative: add the handwritten wordmark in a corner so the brand shows when the title is cut off.

1 · Sender (iOS)

Share lives in the mix’s ⋯ menu on both the conversation and the tape, never as a third bar button. The name page appears only when the sender has no usable name, then the system sheet takes over.

2 · Recipient in the app: SharedMixScreen

Opened by the universal link, or from a "From Ada" row. Works before first run. The listener sees title, tape colour, artwork, songs and Ada’s first name, and nothing of the conversation.

3 · Mixes (iOS)

The shared mix sits among the listener’s own mixes by the time it was saved. It does not count toward the anonymous three-mix cap.

4 · Web /m/<token>

For a recipient without the app. Public: no sign-in, no first run. Light follows the web app’s light plane; dark follows the system.

5 · Web Mixes

A browser that has a session saves the share on open; the Mixes view then shows it like iOS does.

6 · Link preview

Written by the Netlify edge function on /m/* into the app’s HTML. Drawn as iMessage on the recipient’s phone.

Spec notes found while drawing

  1. "Conversation strip’s menu" no longer exists: since one-platform play the strip has fixed buttons and no arrow. Drawn in the header ⋯ menus of the conversation and the tape.
  2. Mixes subtitle. The spec says "From Ada" replaces "the song-count line’s first part", but iOS rows show only relative time ("Today"), no count. Drawn as "From Ada · Today" on iOS and "From Ada · 10 songs · today" on web, where rows do carry a count.
  3. Platform unknown. The spec says the sheet opens on the first action, but not which bar shows before it. Drawn with the Apple pair.
  4. Revoked from a fresh link (never saved): Remove has nothing to remove. Suggest Remove only when a save exists, otherwise Back alone.
  5. iMessage previews generally show og:title and the domain, not og:description, so the mix title may never appear in iMessage. Consider og:title = the mix title and og:description = "Ada shared a mix · 10 songs".
