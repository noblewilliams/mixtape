Material rules

One room, three depthsShell blur 42px, structural panes 32px, controls and status surfaces 18–26px.
Content stays readableText, track rows, and tape artwork do not each get their own blur layer.
Bright edge, soft shadowGlass uses a white hairline and inner top highlight; shadows stay neutral rather than colorful.
Reduced transparencyWhen requested—or when backdrop filtering is unavailable—glass becomes an opaque warm-gray surface with the same boundaries.
Reduced motionNo continuous liquid movement. Any hover depth transition disappears while final states remain clear.
Performance boundaryOnly fixed structural regions blur. Long scrolling lists avoid per-row backdrop filters.
