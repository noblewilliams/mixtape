Flush lists, a visible glow, and the prism as a motif.

Revision 3 applies the September 17 feedback on revision 2. Titles are SF bold; there are no serifs anywhere. Lists sit flush on the background with hairlines, as in Apple Music, and native controls replace drawn ones. The square shape survives only as a motif on artwork tiles and the prism. Home is now empty space above a Playground-style bottom panel that holds the composer, a mic, and idea pills. The background glow is now visible in both themes, the dock is smaller, and the conversation and arrangement screens are shown with how they are reached.

Background · the glow

A vertical fall plus a violet glow rising from the bottom edge, pink at the lower left and blue at the lower right. Light runs warm white to lilac grey; dark runs black to deep violet like the Playground. The glow sits behind the bottom panel and dock so glass has something to refract. Now Playing swaps the glow for the artwork's two dominant colours.

Where the square and the prism appear

Square motif: artwork and cassette tiles at 3 pt radius, the You avatar, the send key. Nothing else is square-cornered; cards, groups, pills and glass are the system's rounded shapes. Prism: composer stripe and focus ring, playback progress, the avatar. One instance per screen.

6 · Answers recorded on approval

  1. Home's open space stays empty with the one hint line.
