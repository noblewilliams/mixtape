Add your music, and Library’s sources

Your notes from TestFlight 1.1.1 (14): “Add your music” on Library should open Add your music (the screen with the Apple Music | Spotify toggle), not Your music. The Add your music layout looks shabby. The Apple Music and Spotify sections at the top of Library need work too. This is a proposal. None of it is built. It ships as an over-the-air patch, since it only touches Dart code.

What you are approving
  1. One way in. “Add music” on Library opens Add your music. Your music is retired. Its only extras were Remove (now on Add your music) and a Browse playlists button (Library already lists your playlists).
  2. Library sources become two flush rows, like Settings: mark, name, status on the right, chevron. They replace the two squeezed halves with a divider. The Add music button moves up beside the title, so Playlists start about where they do today.
  3. Add your music keeps one left edge. Every action sits under its text as a flush row or a button, never floating at the right edge. That means one service block (status, then its actions as rows), then Listening history (a short line, the button, one note, the steps folded underneath).
  4. Tighter type and copy. Three text sizes instead of five. The two long grey paragraphs are cut to one line each. The request steps stay word for word.

1 · Library

Today, the recommended flush rows, the refined-halves alternative, then the states the rows carry.

2 · Add your music · Apple Music

A service block (where you stand, with its actions as rows), then Listening history. The 3 November grammar stays. This pass only changes alignment, spacing and words.

3 · Add your music · Spotify

Same grammar, so the toggle feels like one screen.

4 · What changes, in plain words

Library
  • The big capsule under the title becomes a small glass “Add music” pill beside “Library” (36 pt tall, 44 pt to touch).
  • Sources: two 50 pt rows, flush, with hairlines like every list in the app. The status sits on the right: “✓ Synced 2 Nov”, “Syncing… 42%”, “Not added”.
  • A service you haven’t added shows its mark faded and says “Not added”. It no longer draws a heavy grey disc beside a plum “+ Add music”.
  • Each row opens Add your music on that service’s tab, as today.
Add your music
  • “Sync Apple Music” stops floating at the right of the status. It is a flush action row under the library line, with its glyph in the mark column.
  • Section words drop from 20 to 17.5 pt, with even 26 pt gaps between sections.
  • Remove (from Your music) arrives as a quiet action row once there is something to remove, behind the same “Remove … ?” alert.
  • The tape button loses its rainbow meter stripe here, so the prism isn’t on every button. Open point.
Unchanged
  • The toggle (it still hugs the left margin, per smoke round five).
  • The sync alert and every request step, word for word.
  • The first-run flow, Home’s door and the web app.

5 · Accessibility

  • Library rows are single buttons: “Apple Music, synced 2 November” and “Spotify, not added”. Status is shown with a glyph and words, never colour alone.
  • At large text, the status moves under the name (frames L5 and A6). Nothing truncates, and the Add music pill wraps under the title if it can’t fit beside it.
  • Action rows are 46 pt tall, so the whole row is the target.
  • The faded mark for a service you haven’t added is decoration only. The words “Not added” carry the state.

Recommendation and open points

RecommendedL1 (flush rows) with the Add your music restyle as drawn. Rows read better than halves at every text size, and they need no breakpoint.
AlternativeL2 keeps the side-by-side halves, cleaned up (no divider, smaller marks, faded when not added). It is 40 pt shorter, but the two statuses still compete for one line.
OpenDropping the rainbow meter from the tape button on this screen only. Say “keep the stripe” if you want it everywhere.
