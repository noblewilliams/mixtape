2 · The morph, slowed down

The same three shapes the whole way through. A dot becomes three bars, and the bars become a tick. Shown at 3× size.

Timing:

  • Dot to bars (320 ms): the dot narrows and grows tall into the middle bar while it turns from green to plum. The side bars grow out of its centre and slide 4.5 pt each way, then the bars start their rise and fall.
  • At the same time, "Update" and the small line slide 10 pt left and blur out, and no new words come in.
  • After 1.8 s the app closes.
  • Next start, bars to tick: the bars shrink to short green stubs and gather at the centre, then the check draws itself out from there (340 ms). "Updated" blurs in from the right, holds 2.5 s, and fades.

3 · Accessibility

  • 7 pt is below Apple's 11 pt guidance for legible text, so it only works as a tag. It grows with text size (up to 10.5 pt at the largest setting), and VoiceOver reads it: "Update, restart required. Double-tap to restart mixtape."
  • The small line sits inside the note's 44 pt tap area, so it's tappable too.
  • After the tap nothing is said on screen, so VoiceOver announces "Restarting mixtape. Open it again to finish."
  • Reduced motion: the dot and bars hold still, and each change is a plain cross-fade with no morph, slide or blur.
  • Ships as a patch: it's all drawn in code (rounded rectangles and one stroked path), with no images and no new icons.
