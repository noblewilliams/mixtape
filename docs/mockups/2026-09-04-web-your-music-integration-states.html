Your music, together.

One home for the music you bring to Mixtape. Browse playlists from either source, manage Apple sync, and keep the approved Spotify import flow. This is a review-only extension of the current web app, for everyone who uses a browser.

Open this state full-window

One destination

Proposed: Playlists + Sources. First-time listeners land on Sources; a completed snapshot opens Playlists, even if that collection is empty. The selected section persists during the signed-in visit.

Alternative: always open Sources. Easier to explain initially, but adds a step every time someone returns to browse.

Truthful progress

Reading has counts, not a guessed percentage. Upload percentages describe one stage only. Songs and playlists publish separately: a partial result keeps saved songs and the previous playlists.

A lost completion response means “checking,” not “nothing changed.” All names, dates, counts, and covers here are synthetic.

Scope of this review

Approve shared navigation, source overview, Apple states, and playlist browsing. Interactive controls only switch mockup states; no upload, account authorization, deletion, or playback occurs.

Test light/dark, 320–1240px, large text, and motion/transparency preferences above. The frame uses real viewport breakpoints.

Approval boundary

The existing Spotify approval, documented implementation departures, and shared content-plane approval stay in force. Spotify’s upload, interview, waiting, removal, and mix-output behavior must be preserved. The Spotify scenes below are integration context, not replacement approval for that workspace.

Before implementation, the server needs verified source membership, source/freshness fields, and persistent summary counts; the app needs shared upload ownership and partial/unknown result handling. Those requirements are recorded in the regression matrix, not claimed as implemented.

Not included: memories, mix rename/archive, playlist editing, playlist-origin confirmation, playlist seeding, Apple export uploads, new Spotify playback, or deployment.
