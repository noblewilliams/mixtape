Reel loader: zoom in on the turning hub

Founder note on 1.1.0 (8): the working cassette's hubs do turn, but at these sizes they are 7–19 pt wide, so people see an illustration, not something loading. This board zooms in on one hub, the way the app icon does, so the turning is the main thing you see. It covers the three places the DJ works: Home's "Making your mix", a Mixes row, and the chat's working line.

What you are approving
  • The reel, variant A: the app icon's crop on Home and in chat, and a wider close-up of the same hub in Mixes rows (revised 2026-11-16 after founder review).
  • Where it replaces the working cassette: Home starting state, a working Mixes row, the chat working line.
  • Sizes: 112 pt icon tile on Home, 34 pt icon tile in chat (tilted like today's cassette), and in a Mixes row a closer crop at the cassette's own 68 × 44 size. Same speed as today, one turn every 1.05 s.
  • Covers light and dark mode, reduced motion and large text. Copy and announcements stay as they are.
Not changing: the full cassette stays wherever nothing is loading (empty states, version markers, a finished Mixes row, sign-in, first-run welcome). First run's "making" screen keeps its large 176 pt cassette, since it is the only thing on the screen.
Stop is already shipped: the stop key on Home's composer and in chat is in 1.1.0 (9) from this morning, so it appears below as it already is.

Why it gets missed

The hub's width at each place it spins today, compared with the reel. At 1.05 s per turn, an 11 pt hub with 8 small teeth is hard to see moving out of the corner of your eye.

The reel: two ways to crop

Both are drawn from the same cassette (CassettePainter, viewBox 200 × 128), cropped instead of shrunk. Nothing new is drawn by hand.

A · The icon, turningRecommended

On Home and in chat: exactly the app icon's crop (assets/brand/app-icon.svg, viewBox 108 22 78 78) in the icon's rounded square. In a Mixes row: a wider close-up of the same hub (viewBox 96 29 100 64) at the cassette's own 200 × 128 proportions. It reads as Mixtape at once, keeps the prism stripes and the mix's case colour along the bottom edge, and the hub takes up almost half the tile.

B · Hub in a circle

The hub centred in a circle with a ring of window and stock around it. It looks more like a spinner, but less like the app, and a round shape in a Mixes row clashes with the rectangular cassettes next to it. Why it lost: it is generic, and the brand moment is the point.

Implementation notes

  • A new ReelTile reuses CassettePainter: translate and scale the canvas to a crop rectangle, and clip it. There are two crops: icon (108 22 78 78, 23% continuous corners) and row (96 29 100 64, the cassette's own corner radius). Chat wraps it in the same −0.07 rad tilt the cassette has today. It has one hub controller, stops for reduced motion as CassetteTile does, and is excluded from semantics because the live regions around it already announce the state.
  • Replaced in: home_screen.dart _StartingState, mix_home_row.dart _tile when working, and lyric_turn.dart WorkingCassette. CassetteTile(spinning:) stays for first run and the gallery.
  • Switching between reel and cassette in a row is an instant swap, so a list scroll never animates layout.
