Add your music: label, link, remake

Listeners who skip adding music get mixes picked from Mixtape's catalog, and nothing tells them what their own music would change, or where to add it. This board turns the existing "Not personal yet" text into a way in, and closes the loop when their music lands. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-add-your-music-loop-design.md. The conversation, strip and tape are as approved on 3 November and 9 November. Names and songs are made up; DJ lines show the tone, and the DJ writes its own words.

What this board changes
  1. Label. Every catalog mix keeps "Not personal yet" in the header. The line under the conversation now says what their music would change, with one action named for where they listen.
  2. Link. That action opens the exact step: the Sync Apple Music alert, Spotify's Exportify page, or Choose files when a Spotify export is on its way. It always comes back to the mix, and the line follows the run.
  3. Remake. Once their music is in, the same mix offers "Remake with my music". The DJ rebuilds it as a new version and says how many songs are now theirs. The label goes away.
  4. Honest asks. "My favourites" with no music gets a plain "I don't know your favourites yet". A Spotify listener with likes but no plays gets one "Add my listening history" pill.

Covers iOS (light, dark, largest text) and web (desktop light and dark, phone shell). Replaces the text-only _NotPersonalLine, _NotPersonalBlock and the web panel banner's copy. Nothing changes on Welcome, first-run steps or Home.

1 · The line on a catalog mix

One quiet line under the last turn, then one action. It shows on every catalog mix, because it's an honest label, not a pitch, so it has no close button. It never blocks the mix: Play and Create playlist are untouched. The header's "Not personal yet" stays as the label and isn't a link.

2 · Where the action goes

One router on each platform picks the step from what we already know, and comes back to the mix. Step 1 opens only when we don't know where they listen.

Apple Music, no library yetiOS: the existing Sync Apple Music alert, over the conversation. If they've played a mix through Apple Music, the access prompt is already answered, so it's one tap. Web: Step 3's sync page.
Spotify, nothing in yetStep 3's Exportify page (first-run board B6), unchanged, in Library mode. Back or Done returns to this mix.
Spotify export requestedThe import sheet with Choose files, for when Spotify's email arrives.
Not knownStep 1, "Which do you use?", in Library mode.
"Add my listening history" (section 4)Step 3's data export page: Spotify's, or "Upload an Apple export".

3 · Remake with my music

The payoff. Once their music is in, every catalog mix offers to rebuild itself from it, whenever they open it again. Spotify's export can take days, so this can't depend on them waiting on the page. The remake is a normal turn and makes a new version, so the catalog version stays in Versions.

4 · When the request needs their music

Requests like "my favourites" or "songs I love" can't come from the catalog. The DJ says that plainly instead of passing a catalog mix off as their favourites.

5 · Web

The same loop in the web's own drawing: lyric turns, hairline pills, the amber banner. On desktop the mix panel sits beside the conversation, so the action appears once, in the conversation. The banner keeps the label. In the phone shell the panel covers the conversation, so the banner carries the action too.
