Library sources, sync and Add your music

Founder smoke notes from build 202611030734. Four surfaces: the Library title buttons, the sources strip above Playlists, the sync confirmation (sheet → centred alert), and the Apple pane of Add your music. This is a proposal; none of it is built.

What you are approving
  1. Library title buttons get words. Sync (two arrows, the universal "refresh from source" glyph) and Your music. The More (…) menu and its one-row sheet are gone.
  2. Sources strip becomes one 62 pt row of two halves, each a service mark, the service name and a status line: ✓ Synced 2 Nov or + Add music. Roughly half the height of today's chip-and-link row.
  3. Sync is a centred alert, not a sheet, and it knows it's a re-sync: "Sync Apple Music again?" with the last date. First-time copy only for people who've never synced.
  4. Add your music, Apple pane leads with where you stand (library synced, history added or not), then "Listening history" as its own section with Choose your Apple file in plain sight. The how-to-request steps fold under it, for people who haven't asked Apple yet.
  5. The Apple half of the strip opens the Apple pane (today it opens Your music). That's the second door to the history file.
One conflict to flag. You asked for "images like icons" for Spotify and Apple Music. The 18 Sept decision is single-tone service marks, never the app icons, so the board uses the existing marks larger, in ink. If you want brand-colour icons (red Apple Music tile, green Spotify disc), say so and I'll add that as a superseding decision.

1 · Library

Before and after, then the states the strip has to carry.

2 · Sync confirmation

A centred alert in the native style (the same alert Your music uses for Remove). It stays up while it runs; "Hide" leaves the sync running and the strip shows the progress.

3 · Add your music · Apple Music

One grammar for the pane: where you stand (status row with its one action) → Listening history (choose the file first, how-to-request folded under it). No inset box; flush sections like the rest of the app.

4 · Add your music · Spotify

Same grammar so the toggle feels like one screen: a status row, then Saved music (Exportify) and Listening history (the official request). The step copy is unchanged.

5 · Accessibility

  • Title buttons: accessible names "Sync Apple Music" and "Your music"; 44 pt targets (40 pt glass inside). At large text the labels stay; "Library" wraps above them if needed (frame L5).
  • Strip halves are single buttons: "Apple Music, synced 2 November" / "Spotify, not added. Add music". Status never relies on colour: tick + words, warning glyph + words, spinner + percentage.
  • Alert: progress is announced as "Syncing, 42 percent" at most every 10 percent; done and failed states are announced as their titles.
  • Large text stacks the strip into two full-width rows; steps wrap freely.

Recommendation and open points

RecommendedEverything above as drawn. The one judgement call is the strip opening Add your music (not Your music): it puts the history file one tap from Library, and Your music stays one tap away from the title.
Intentionally unchangedYour music screen itself, the Spotify step copy, the first-run flow (it has its own sync screen), the web app.
OpenSpotify-only listeners: the Sync button would ask for Apple Music access. I'd hide Sync for anyone without an Apple Music source and keep Your music alone. Say if you'd rather keep it for everyone.
