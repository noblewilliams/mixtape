Native iOS and web · album picks and Listen later · revision 1 · synthetic data

One album, chosen for you.

Beside a mix, the DJ can answer a prompt with one album. The composer gains the app-wide segmented control with Mix and Album; the conversation shows an album card where the tape card would be; the album can be played now or saved to a Listen later shelf in Mixes. This board draws every state the spec names, on both platforms, taken from the approved shell, Home, conversation, Mixes and "New to you" boards. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-20-album-picks-design.md.

Nothing here is built. Every frame is a plan. Album names, artists, artwork and the DJ's replies are synthetic. Artwork is drawn as plain gradients; the product shows the real cover from the Apple catalogue.
Planned

1 · The composer: Mix or Album

The one app-wide segmented control, in the Home panel above the composer. It is a mode: it resets to Mix on every launch and is never a remembered setting. Under Album, the Shape chip is gone and no shape is sent. Three starter pills change with the mode.

3 · The album card

Where a mix shows the tape card, an album session shows the album: cover, title, artist, a meta line with the record's type, year, songs and length, the "why" line, and the actions. Flush on the conversation, no box, like the tape card.

Web

4 · Listen later, in Mixes

A third segment beside Active and Archived. Flush rows with the cover in the 60 pt tile, the title, and "artist · type · year". Tapping a row opens its album session (A2), where play-now and refinement already live. Listened rows sit below the unlistened ones under their own heading, muted, and stay on the shelf.

5 · Honest failures

The DJ never shows an album it could not verify in the Apple catalogue, and never invents one. These are plain replies in the conversation, with the refinements the listener can take.

7 · What you are approving

  • The composer: Mix | Album in the app-wide segmented control, compact (26 pt) above the composer on native and in the tool row on web; Shape hidden under Album; the visible nudge with a way back; no control for anonymous listeners; album starter pills. Recommended as drawn
  • The album card: flush, cover at 112 pt (96 px web), meta line with the type label, one "why" line, Play now as the tape button, Listen later as a bookmark chip, two runners-up collapsed under a 44 pt row, passed-over picks as muted rows in the transcript, the "Open in Spotify" text action for Spotify-only listeners.
  • Listen later: a third segment in Mixes (native) and a third tab (web); flush rows; listened rows under their own heading, muted, with a status word; expand for the "why"; swipe to remove with Undo; the manual "Mark listened"; the empty state.
  • Honest failures: nothing verified, comfort with an empty shelf, and the failed turn, as plain replies with pills.

Alternatives considered and not drawn: a list of three albums instead of one pick (loses the decision the DJ is meant to make; runners-up carry the choice without it); Listen later as its own tab in the shell (a fifth tab for a shelf most sessions will not touch); the nudge inferring Album silently (the listener would not know what they were about to get).

Covered: native light and dark, 1.5x text, the web panel at 760 px and 390 px in light and dark, the Mixes shelf on both platforms, loading, saved, unsaved, passed over, Spotify-only, empty and the three failures.

Not covered, on purpose: the Album actions menu contents (Rename, Archive, as a mix); listened-from-playback, which waits for album playback evidence; "Make a mix around this album"; any change to the Now Playing sheet, which already shows whatever Apple Music is playing.
