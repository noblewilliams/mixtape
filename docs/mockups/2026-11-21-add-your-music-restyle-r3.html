Mixtape · iOS · state board · revision 3 · 21 November 2026

Add your music and Library, compact, with the official icons

Your notes on revision 2: the icons should be smaller, the sections more compact on both screens, and we should use the real logo files instead of drawing them. Every icon on this board is now the official file: Apple's Apple_Music_Icon_RGB_sm (2020 icon pack) and Spotify's Primary_Logo_Black_RGB / White_RGB (2024 icon pack). Their path data is used verbatim. This is still a proposal. None of it is built.

What changed since revision 2
  1. Official files, nothing redrawn. Apple Music is its colour tile. Spotify is its one-colour icon, black on light and white on dark, because Spotify allows its green icon only on a pure black or white background, and our gradient is neither.
  2. No grey not-added icon. Both brands forbid recolouring. A service you haven't added keeps its real icon, and the words “Not added” carry the state.
  3. Smaller. The icons are 22 pt in Library rows (Spotify's minimum is 21 px) and 32 pt on Add your music. They were 30 and 56.
  4. More compact. Library rows go from 52 to 44 pt.
    • Add your music's header is now one line: icon, name, “1 of 2 added” and a small meter.
    • Done steps fit on one line, with their actions moved to the right.
    • The open step's text, button and how-to sit closer together. A1 is about 40% shorter than in revision 2.

5 · Accessibility and brand rules

  • Brand icons are decoration (excluded from semantics), because the row and header already name the service.
  • Done steps read as “Step 1, Library, done, synced 2 November”. Their actions stay buttons with 44 pt targets, even though they look small.
  • At the largest text the done-step actions drop under the status (A6), and nothing truncates.
  • Spotify: the icon is never under 21 px, keeps clear space of half its height, and is the monochrome version off black or white backgrounds. Apple: the icon is unaltered, from the approved colour, black or white versions.

Recommendation

RecommendedEverything as drawn. This revision supersedes the 18 September single-tone rule for these two services (official files instead of drawn marks), and that change gets a decision entry.
Ships as a patchThe icons are embedded as code from the official files, not as new asset files, because over-the-air patches only carry Dart code. The original files are kept in docs/brand/ as the source.
