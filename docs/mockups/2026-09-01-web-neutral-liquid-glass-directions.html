Liquid glass revision · shared content plane

Let the content move beneath the glass.

Chat, Home, and the rest of the app share one softly painted content plane. The left and right sidebars float above it as translucent glass, revealing a quieter trace of the same motion instead of animating their own gradients.

Recommendation: C · One moving plane. The paint follows an organic four-point path over 10 seconds. It never cycles hue: the center shows the motion clearly, while the overlaid sidebars reduce it to a subtle reflection in both light and dark mode.
What the references are doing

Color belongs to content, not the room.

The useful design lesson is the division of labor: a quiet canvas makes artwork feel richer, while translucent navigation and floating controls remain legible because they sit above real content.

Near-white canvasLarge content areas stay neutral with strong black hierarchy and generous breathing room.
Softly tinted chromeDesktop sidebars can lean faintly lilac or rose without becoming decorative color fields.
Artwork owns saturationAlbum and tape art provide the strong blues, reds, yellows, and greens.
Glass floats over contentBlur is easiest to read on temporary or persistent chrome such as a player, navigation bar, drawer, or composer.

Dark material contract

Dark is designed, not inverted

CanvasDeep graphite #151518, not pure black.
Paint identityAn active mix keeps its stable color; Home uses the same quiet house slate as light mode.
Layer orderThe content plane moves; smoky sidebars and top chrome sit above it with no independent animation.
ComposerDark neutral interior, unchanged colorful focus border, readable white input text.
Reduced motionLight and dark both freeze at a balanced static frame.
System settingThe shipped theme follows the system color preference unless the product later adds a manual override.
What approval coversLight and dark desktop session/Home, mobile session/Home, the shared 10-second content-plane path, and static accessibility fallbacks. Dark dialogs and authentication inherit neutral dark glass and contain no mix paint.
