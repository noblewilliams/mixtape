Use the three-line handle to reorder. Swipe left with one finger on touch or two fingers on a trackpad. The remove button appears and stretches during the gesture. Release past 65 percent to remove. Every reorder or removal has a three-second Undo. Hover or focus a row to hear why it belongs.

Three-line handleAlways on the far right, with three equal bars lightly tinted in Mixtape’s blue, green, and pink. It strengthens on hover and focus.
Live swipeThe X appears under the pointer and its coral surface stretches with distance. Release past 65% to remove; shorter swipes settle open.
Two-finger trackpadHorizontal wheel input mirrors the touch gesture, so two fingers are enough. Vertical scrolling remains untouched.
Undo for 3 secondsRemoval and reorder show a flush icon-and-text Undo action. It quietly disappears after three seconds with no progress indicator.
Reason on hoverA third line fades in inside reserved space, so adjacent rows and actions do not jump.

Responsive and accessible behavior
Desktop ≥1020px
The 468px rail stays persistent. One-finger pointer drag and two-finger trackpad swipe share the same 65% threshold.
Tablet and mobile
The same rail becomes a nearly full-height sheet. Swipe and reorder handles remain separate 44px targets.
Keyboard
Arrow keys on the handle reorder. Delete on a focused row reveals the X; Escape closes it. Status text announces versioned results.
Undo result
Reorder and removal can be reversed for three seconds. The flush icon-and-text action quietly expires without a progress bar.
Approval boundary: this locks the right rail width, artwork-led rows, three-line handle, hover/focus reason, live proportional swipe, 65% release-to-remove, one-finger touch and two-finger trackpad input, three-second single-level Undo, sticky playback actions, stale-order recovery, and mobile-sheet behavior. Track menus remain outside this approval.
