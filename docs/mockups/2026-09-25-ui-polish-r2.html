A cassette that reads at a glance.

The app icon is the cassette itself, cropped edge to edge around a reel and its coloured stripes. The favicon keeps the small cassette silhouette. The handwritten wordmark stays unchanged.

Recommendation: A for the app; the small cassette for web.

A leaves more of the coloured stripes beside the reel. B moves closer to the reel, filling more of the icon with its shape. Neither places a complete cassette inside a separate container: the operating system masks the full-bleed artwork. The favicon remains a complete cassette. Launcher exports follow approval.

Every state has a next step.

Compare the same situation on the native app and the web. Switch themes, enlarge the text, or narrow the web preview. Actions stay local to this board.

Quiet feedback. Clear recovery.

Success can leave quietly.

Use a short toast with a slim cassette-prism edge after confirmation. One at a time. No toast when the changed screen already makes success obvious. Screen readers receive a polite announcement.

Recovery stays in reach.

A failed page load stays on the page. A failed save stays beside its field. Preserve entered text. A connection failure must never masquerade as an empty library.

Keep the useful screen.

If a refresh fails, keep the last loaded content with a retry message. Feedback sits above the player and navigation; it never replaces either.
