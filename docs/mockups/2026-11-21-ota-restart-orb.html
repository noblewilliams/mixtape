The update note: "New update (restart)" with a green orb

Your direction: an editorial label, "New update (restart)", next to a soft green orb that breathes and looks a little 3D. On tap, the orb turns into the level bars. The words slide a short way to the side and blur out, and the next words blur and slide in. iOS still can't reopen the app, so after the tap it closes (decision 2026-11-20). Tap a note to play it.

One conflict to flag. "Editorial" usually means a serif, but your 17 September rule is no serifs, SF only (Noteworthy for the wordmark is the one exception). E1 keeps SF and gets the editorial feel from weight and an italic: New update (restart). E2 uses Apple's own serif, New York. Choosing E2 replaces the rule for this one note.
