2 · Spotify request flow — two directions

The decision: where the eight steps live while the listener waits days for Spotify. A keeps them in a one-time checklist dialog. B (recommended) gives them a page under Your music that stays available, holds the “I’ve requested it” checkpoint, shows the elapsed wait, and becomes the import page when a file arrives. The email-confirmation step is the one most people miss, so it is set in bold in both.

Direction A · one-time checklist dialog

Spotify

Get your listening data

  1. Open spotify.com/account/privacy and log in with the account that has your listening history. A laptop is easier than a phone for this part.
  2. Scroll to Download your data.
  3. Select Account data and Extended streaming history. Leave Technical log information unselected.
  4. Press Request data.
  5. Check your email. Spotify sends a confirmation message first. Open it and press Confirm. Nothing is prepared until you do, and this is the step most people miss.
  6. Wait. The two packages arrive as separate emails, each with a Download button, usually within days; the extended history can take up to 30. Each link expires after about two weeks, so download it when you see it.
  7. Save the ZIPs as they are. Don’t unzip them.
  8. Come back to Mixtape and give it each ZIP as it arrives. You don’t have to wait for both.

No stripe travel and no pulsing chip. The chip text carries the whole state; the bar is a plain filled band.

  • Status chip is the live region; announcements: “Uploading, 62 percent, days 3 of 4”, “Import complete, 4,812 tracks”, “Couldn’t read this export”.
  • Every “Open in Spotify” link has a 44px target (dashed outline shows the hit area in frame 6) and an accessible name naming the track.
  • The private-sessions toggle is a real switch with an accessible name and its default state announced.
  • Status is never colour alone: chips carry text, the banner carries text, ignored files carry the word “ignored” or “never read”.

9 · The decision

Recommended — B: request page under Your music with a living waiting cardThe steps stay findable for the days the listener waits, the checkpoint and elapsed time live where the file will be dropped, and the same page becomes the import page and the sources view. No second navigation destination, no modal that disappears with the instructions in it.
Rejected — A: one-time checklist dialogSmaller build, but the instructions vanish when dismissed, the listener has no place to see how long they have waited, and the drop zone would need its own home anyway. It also competes with the pending Your music board for the same space.

Intentionally unresolved: exact placement of the request page relative to the Apple states in the pending Your music board; the transfer tool named in the handoff (TuneMyMusic or Soundiiz, decided at build time by whichever accepts pasted text without an account); whether the demo tape opens in place or as a new session.
