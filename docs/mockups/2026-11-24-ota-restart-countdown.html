Restart countdown: a heads-up before the app closes to update

On device, tapping "Update" shows the bars for 1.8 seconds and then the app is just gone, which feels like a crash. iOS has no way for an app to restart itself, so it still has to close. This board makes the close something the listener can see coming and stay in control of.

Decisions for you:
  • Where the countdown shows: A, a glass popover that grows out of the Update note (recommended), or B, a modal in the middle of the screen.
  • Copy on the card and in the reopen notification (section 3).
  • Quiet update when leaving the app, and the cases where it holds off (section 4).

1 · A: popover from the note recommended

Tap "Update" in the frames below. The note gives way to a glass popover anchored where it sat: "Restarting in 5", counting down to 1, with Cancel and Restart now. Cancel puts the note back. At 0 the bars play for a moment and the app closes. Popovers anchored to their control are how menus already work across the app (2026-11-03), so this is a pattern listeners already know.

2 · B: centred modal

The same card and words in the middle of the screen, over a light scrim. It's harder to miss, but it blocks the app for 5 seconds and makes a quiet update feel like an alert. It doesn't grow out of anything, so the link between tapping "Update" and the card is weaker.

3 · After the close: the reopen notification

Just before closing, the app schedules a local notification for one second later. The app is closed when it arrives, so iOS shows it as a normal banner. Tapping it opens mixtape, which plays the "Updated" tick that already ships. The plugin that sends it is already in the 1.2.0 binary, so this can go out as a patch.

OptionTitleBody
N1 recommendedmixtape is up to dateTap to jump back in.
N2Update finishedOpen mixtape to pick up where you left off.
N3mixtape updatedTap to reopen.

The notification doesn't say "where you left off" unless that's true. A mix being made keeps going on the server, but playback has stopped. N2 is only fair if Home reopens on what they were looking at, which it doesn't today.

4 · Quiet update when leaving the app (no UI)

Once a patch is waiting and the listener leaves mixtape, the app closes itself in the background. They never see it happen. Next time they open it, it's a fresh start with the patch, and the "Updated" tick plays as usual.

1 · Patch downloadedThe note shows "Update". Nothing else happens yet.
2 · Listener leaves the appHome button, app switcher or locking the phone. The app waits 3 s in the background so a quick glance at another app doesn't cost them anything.
3 · Holds off if…music is playing from mixtape, a voice note is recording, an import is running, or a mix is mid-send. It tries again on the next trip to the background.
4 · Closes quietlyNo notification for this path. Nothing seen to close, so nothing to reopen.
5 · Next openA cold start with the patch: "Updated" plays in the note.

The tradeoff: iOS usually keeps an app in memory for a while, and returning to it normally lands exactly where you were. On this one return after an update, the listener lands on Home instead. That happens once per patch.

5 · Accessibility and edge cases

  • VoiceOver: when the card opens, focus moves to it and VoiceOver hears "Restarting mixtape in 5 seconds to finish the update. Restart now, or Cancel." It doesn't read every second. With VoiceOver on, the countdown doesn't run by itself. The card waits for Restart now, because a 5-second deadline is too short to hear the card and find the button.
  • Haptics: the light click on the tap (as now), and none per second. A tick every second would feel like an alarm.
  • Leaving during the countdown (home swipe) cancels it, and the quiet background update from section 4 takes over.
  • Tapping outside the popover does nothing. It's a 5-second decision, and a stray tap shouldn't decide it. Cancel is the only way out.
  • Two tabs: the popover belongs to the note the listener tapped (Home or You). The countdown lives in the shared update state, so switching tabs mid-count doesn't start a second one.
  • Restart, Cancel and the state are all words. The draining line only adds to the number.
