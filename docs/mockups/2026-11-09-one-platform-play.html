One platform, fixed mix actions

Answers a TestFlight recording from 9 November: a listener made a mix and couldn't play it. Play failed three times with a vague Apple Music error, and the arrow menu renamed and hid buttons as they tried things. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-09-one-platform-play-design.md. Everything else in the 3 November approval stays. Names and songs are made up.

What this board changes
  1. Ask once where they listen. If we don't already know, a sheet asks Apple Music or Spotify the first time the app opens after first run. Anyone who picked a service at sign-up never sees it.
  2. No arrow. Split Play goes away. The bar is fixed by platform and is the same in the conversation and on the tape.
  3. Apple Music: Play + Create playlist. Play asks for Apple Music access on the tap if needed. If Mixtape's player still can't start, the mix plays in the Music app instead.
  4. Spotify: Create playlist only. No Play. "Play on Spotify" was really making a playlist through TuneMyMusic, so the button now says that.
  5. You → Listen on has two choices, Apple Music or Spotify.

Supersedes the 3 November split Play (winner B) and the 3 November "Play on Spotify" label. Covers iOS, light and dark, default and largest text. Web is out of scope.

1 · Ask once

A sheet over Home, the first time the shell appears after first run, only when nothing tells us the platform. It has two equal answers, no grab handle, and the scrim doesn't close it, because the answer decides every mix's buttons. The answer is saved on the device. Apple Music is listed first because it is the one platform Mixtape can play.

2 · Apple Music listener

Play and Create playlist, side by side, in the strip and on the tape. Nothing on the bar changes except Play becoming Playing while Mixtape's player is on this mix. To give both buttons room, the strip puts them on their own row under the title (A). Squeezing them beside the title (B) cuts most mix names to a few letters.

3 · Play, when something is in the way

Play always ends in music or an honest reason. The vague "Check access and your subscription" screen is gone.

4 · Spotify listener

Mixtape can't play on Spotify, so there is no Play. The one action makes the playlist (copy the songs, open TuneMyMusic), with the Spotify mark so it's clear where it goes. Rows open the song in Spotify.

5 · You → Listen on

The row keeps its place and icon. It now has two choices; the "Apple Music, in Mixtape" vs "Apple Music app" split is gone, because Play falls back to the Music app on its own. Changing it changes every mix's bar right away.

6 · One-artist mixes (no new UI)

Server-side: a request for one artist's own music ("SZA songs", "just Drake", "Cole I can rap to") builds from that artist only, features included. "Like X" or "X vibes" still means similar artists. When we can find fewer songs than asked for, the DJ says so instead of padding with other artists.

  2. The ask-once sheet can't be dismissed without an answer.
  3. Spotify listeners get no Play; their one action is "Create playlist" with the Spotify mark.
  4. Not decided here: web parity (follow-up).
