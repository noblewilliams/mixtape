Add your music, redesigned, and nicer service icons

Your notes on revision 1: “use L1 but nicer icons for Apple Music and Spotify as well as the tick and the warning icon”, and Add your music needs a proper redesign, not a tidy-up. L1 (flush rows, Add music beside the title, Your music retired, Remove moved to Add your music) is locked. This is still a proposal. None of it is built.

What you are approving
  1. Service icons: the real brand marks (I1). Apple Music as its red tile with the white note, and Spotify as the green disc with black waves. A service you haven't added shows its icon in grey. This reverses the 18 September rule (“single-tone marks, never the app icons”), so it gets a superseding decision entry.
  2. Tick and warning become filled symbols, matching the system: a green filled circle with a white check, and an amber filled triangle with a white “!”. The thin outline circles go away.
  3. Add your music becomes a two-step checklist per service. At the top is a hero (the big brand icon, the service name, “1 of 2 added” and a two-segment meter). Below it are two steps:
    • Apple Music: Library, then Listening history.
    • Spotify: Saved music, then Listening history.
    A step that's done shows a filled tick, one status line and small text actions. The step you're on is open, with its words, its one button and the how-to fold.

1 · Icons

Close-up at the sizes they ship: 30 pt in Library rows, 56 pt on the hero, 15 pt status glyphs. I2 is the fallback if you'd rather keep the 18 September single-tone rule.

2 · Library (L1 with the new icons)

3 · Add your music · Apple Music

The hero says how far along you are. The checklist says what's next. Only the step you're on is open. Remove (from Your music) sits with a step once there's something to remove, behind today's “Remove … ?” alert.

4 · Add your music · Spotify

Same shape. The Exportify buttons stay on opposite edges (smoke round five). The request steps are word for word.

5 · Accessibility

  • Hero: “Apple Music, 1 of 2 added.” Each step is a container: “Step 1, Library, done, synced 2 November”, or “Step 2, Listening history, optional, not added”.
  • Done, open and needs-attention never rely on colour. The tick, number and “!” glyph are paired with words, and the grey not-added icon also says “Not added”.
  • Brand icons are decoration (excluded from semantics), because the row and hero already name the service.
  • At the largest text the hero stacks the icon above the name, every line wraps, and the step buttons go full width with a 44 pt target (A7).
  • Filled-glyph contrast: white on #34A853 is 3.2:1 as a graphic, which meets the 3:1 rule for non-text. The words beside it use the darker ok ink.

Recommendation and open points

RecommendedI1 brand icons, filled status glyphs, and the checklist redesign as drawn.
Brand useBoth services allow their marks for linking to their service. Apple asks that its icon not be altered (the grey not-added state is a tint, not a redraw). If you'd rather not touch that, the not-added state can be the full-colour icon with “Not added” beside it.
OpenThe hero meter (two short segments) is the one new decoration. Say “no meter” and the hero keeps only the words.
