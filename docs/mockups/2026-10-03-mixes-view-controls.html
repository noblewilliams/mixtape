Mixes: shared tabs, icon view switch, list / grid / closet, centred empty states, new theme switch

Design board for approval. Nothing here is shipped. The frame below is live: click the tabs, the three view icons, and the theme switch at the bottom of the sidebar. Use the controls to step through states. All mix names are made up.

Each tile is its own switch, so you can replay the motion without changing the frame above. Tick “Slow motion” to see the sequence.

A · Reel sunRecommended
The sun is the cassette's reel hub: a ring, a spindle hole and eight teeth. Going dark, the reel spins and the teeth wind in, the hub fills, a shadow slides across to leave a crescent, then two stars pop. Going light runs it backwards and the teeth spring out. The page itself changes as a circle opening from the switch.

B · Flip the tape
Side A is day, side B is night. The tape turns over on its long edge. Charming, but it is a second cassette on a screen already full of them, and the detail is hard to read at 50 px.

What you are approving

  • Tabs. One shared Tabs component, taken from Library's Playlists / Sources tabs, used on both pages. Labels shorten to “Active” and “Archived”; the page title stays “Mixes” on both tabs so nothing jumps. The count line reads “7 mixes” or “2 archived mixes” (and “1 mix”, not “1 mixes”).
  • View switch. Three icons, List, Grid and Closet, in one quiet outlined group. The selected view uses the same soft fill as the current row in the sidebar instead of the solid plum block. Each has a tooltip and a spoken name (“List view”, pressed).
  • List. Rows start at the page gutter with no extra indent, hairlines run the full content width, the first row sits directly under the toolbar, and row padding drops from 14 px to 10 px. The 80 px cassette from the polish approval stays.
  • Grid (new). Unboxed cassettes on the page, name and song count underneath, on the same column rhythm as Library's playlist grid. Two columns on a phone-width window.
  • Empty and error states. Centred both ways in the space under the toolbar, in every view. Same art, wording and single action as today.
  • Closet. Two options, switch them with the “Closet” control. ARecommended drops the grey wall and stands the shelves on the app's own background, edge to edge like the list's hairlines, with the tapes starting at the page gutter. It reads as part of the page, the same way the cassettes do in List and Grid. B keeps the wall and makes it the whole page: it runs edge to edge under the toolbar and down to the bottom of the window, with spare shelves waiting. Richer, but a heavy slab on the light theme. Tape, shelf, plant and bird art is unchanged in both; A gives up the wall's sunlight streaks.
  • Theme switch. A single 44 px icon button under Settings, lined up with the nav icons, replacing the pill. Option A or B above.

Covered, and not covered

  • Covered: web only, light and dark, desktop and 390 px, larger text, reduced motion (switch and page change instantly, tapes do not lift), loading, empty, archived empty, refresh failed, could not load, one mix, a long name, 34 mixes.
  • The circular page reveal needs a browser with View Transitions. Elsewhere the theme changes instantly and the icon still animates.
  • Not decided here: a way to go back to “match my system” after choosing a theme (today the first click locks your choice). Row menu contents, rename, archive and tape settings are unchanged. Native is unchanged.
  • This would supersede the “List / Closet” text switch from the 2 October navigation approval, the pill theme switch, and the centred rack box from the 27 September rack approval. It also reopens “square tape tiles” rejected on 25 September, as an unboxed grid.
