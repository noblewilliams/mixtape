Which songs are new to you.

A mix may now hold a few songs the listener does not own. Each one needs a mark in the song list, and creating a playlist from such a mix needs one honest line, because it adds those songs to their Apple Music library. Nothing else on these screens changes. All rows, colours and type below are taken from the shipped queue panel and arrangement screen.

Decision asked: where the mark sits. Variant A puts the words "New to you" at the end of the artist line. Variant B puts a small "NEW" after the title. A is recommended: it is the quieter of the two, it follows the native rule that status is a coloured word inside the subtitle and never a box, and it leaves the title alone. Its cost is that a long artist name is shortened to make room. B is easier to spot when scanning titles, and its cost is that it shortens the title instead and reads as a label competing with it.

Rules this board keeps: no box, pill, border, dot or icon on the row; no sparkle glyph; the mark is words, so it never relies on colour; it is not a control, so targets and row height do not change; nothing animates.

Colour: the mark uses the accent ink already in each theme (plum on light, near-white on dark) at a heavier weight than the artist. It is words in every theme, so it does not depend on colour. No motion is added, so the reduced-motion reading is identical.

7 · Left open on purpose

The count of new songs is not added to the mix's header or meta line; the DJ already says it in the conversation. The iOS alert keeps its two existing titles ("Save as playlist" on this screen, "Create playlist" from the conversation). Spotify-only listeners do not receive new songs yet, so the Open link beside a mark in W2 and W3 is drawn for when they do.
