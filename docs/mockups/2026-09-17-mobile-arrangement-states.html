The tape, song by song.

The arrangement is the ordered mix behind a conversation: reorder, remove, read the DJ's reasons, then play it here, send it to Music, or turn it into a playlist. Its frame was approved with the shell: glass clusters, small title, mix actions at the top, flush track rows with square art and drag grips, tab bar hidden. This board draws its states from the current screen, including the Spotify listener's version and every handoff result.

One gap and one rename, both stated. Removing a song has no Undo in the native code today; the shell approval asks for one and it is drawn here as the intended behaviour. The Play in Mixtape button is labelled "Play now" per the locked vocabulary; Send to Music remains the system-player handoff.
