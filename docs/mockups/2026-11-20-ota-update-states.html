Over-the-air updates: a tiny note in the title row

Founder notes so far: the updating screen shouldn't take over the whole screen, so a small status sits on the same row as the "Home" title and on the "You" title row (revision 1). Revision 2: the ready word is "Update available", and the loader is no longer a cassette, because there's too much tape on screen already. The update itself works as before (decision 2026-11-20): a patch downloads quietly, runs from the next start, and tapping the note closes the app so it starts on the new version.

What you are approving
  • The note: one 12 pt semibold line plus a 14 pt glyph pinned to the right edge of the Home and You title rows. 26 pt drawn, 44 pt to touch. No capsule, no glow, no prism, no tape.
  • Glyphs: a 7 pt record dot when an update is available (the transport "record" mark, the same plum as the Mixes ready dot) · loader A, three level bars while updating · a check when it's done.
  • Four states: nothing (almost always) · Update available · Updating after the tap (1.8 s, then the app closes) · on the next start Updating for 1.4 s, then Updated in green for 2.5 s, then a 320 ms fade.
  • Removed: the full-screen updating screen and the "A new version of mixtape is ready · Restart" toast.
  • Covers light and dark, 200% text, 320 and 390 pt widths, reduced motion. Mixes and Library are unchanged.
A departure to note: the house rule says loading is "the cassette with its hubs turning". This note is the one exception, at your request; everywhere else keeps the reel.
No visual change, but part of this work: the hidden mini-player was taking taps above the tab bar. Fixed, see the last section.

Fixed, nothing to see: the hidden mini-player's taps

Before

With nothing playing, the mini-player is hidden, but its play and next buttons still answered taps in a 44 pt patch just above the tab bar on the right. Anything Flutter drew there, a toast's action or the right side of a list row, did nothing.

After

The hidden pill answers no taps (MiniPlayerView.hitTest checks it is visible first). Taps there reach the screen again. Checked in the simulator on iOS 26.5; Undo toasts were very likely hit too but are not checked on a device.

Implementation notes

  • UpdateStatusNote (new) reads appUpdateProvider and draws the line plus its glyph: record dot, three level bars (one 900 ms ease-in-out loop, bars offset by a third each, still under reduced motion), or CupertinoIcons.checkmark. It goes in the large-title row of home_screen.dart and you_tab.dart through LargeTitleScaffold's trailing slot, falling back to the below-title accessory when it doesn't fit.
  • UpdatingOverlay, UpdatingScreen and UpdateReadyPrompt are deleted, with the shell's dock-hiding and GlassOcclusion.coverAll, which only existed for the full screen. The launch no longer covers anything while it reads the recorded patch.
  • Timings: closing 1.8 s, applying 1.4 s, updated 2.5 s, fade 320 ms ease-out, word swap 280 ms fade-through.
  • Signed-out listeners (first run) have no title row; for them the patch runs from the next start with no note. Say if they need a place too.
