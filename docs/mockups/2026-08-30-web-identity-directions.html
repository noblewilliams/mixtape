Approval board · visual identity exploration

Four ways a personal DJ could feel.

Every direction uses the same real product: start from a moment, talk with the DJ, shape the current queue, hand playback to Apple Music, and keep previous sessions. The decision here is the emotional world—not the feature set.

Audience
A music lover who wants the right queue without building it by hand.
Single job
Turn one honest sentence into a queue that feels personally selected.
Constant across concepts
Sessions-first structure, no album art assumption, long-turn state, errors, responsive layout.

Choose one identity or name specific elements to combine.
A Warm · considered · human

Listening Room

A quiet room, a handwritten liner note, and a DJ who has time for the whole record. The interface feels collected rather than generated: warm paper surfaces, walnut shadows, amber controls, and literary titles.

Recommended starting pointBest match for “personal DJ” and the mixtape name. It carries warmth without relying on album artwork, but needs disciplined typography to avoid becoming nostalgic or overly precious.

Signal Desk

The DJ booth as a control surface. Hard edges, channel labels, signal orange, level meters, and factual type make the system feel responsive and capable. Every change reads like a live adjustment to the mix.

Strongest interaction languageExcellent for queues, controls, and transparent system state. The risk is emotional distance: it can feel like production software unless the writing stays warm.

Nocturne

A session feels like entering a scene. Deep violet fields, moonlit cyan, generous curves, and an orbital track marker make mood—not machinery—the organising principle.

Most emotionally expressiveStrongest fit for night drives, heartbreak, focus, and memory prompts. It is less neutral across daytime contexts and must avoid becoming a generic neon streaming app.

Field Index

A working music collection: catalog cards, stamped session labels, blue index numbers, red action tabs, and visible structure. The metaphor is crate-digging and handwritten classification—not a retro cassette player.

Most distinctive systemHighly ownable and excellent for session history. Its deliberate roughness is less luxurious, and responsive implementation needs precision so dense details remain calm.

B · Signal Desk

Choose this if queue manipulation and visible system response should define the brand.

Mood-forward
